# First Meeting Prep — Army Pink Escape Club

## Goal
Lock in enough context to start building the Discord server structure, onboarding flow, and content templates by end of week 1.

---

## Questions to Ask

### 1. Program & Audience
- Who is the Escape Club for — current Freedom Rides users, alumni, or a broader survivor community?
- What's the relationship between the Escape Club and the Pathway to Freedom program?
- Are members self-referred, or do they come through partner orgs / hotlines?
- What stage of their journey are most members in? (crisis, transitioning, rebuilding, mixed?)
- Approximate expected membership size at launch? Growth expectations?

### 2. Server Purpose & Goals
- What's the #1 thing you want members to feel when they join this server?
- Is this primarily for peer support, resource access, wellness programming, or all three?
- Will there be live events (wellness sessions, guest speakers, Q&As)?
- Are there specific outcomes you're measuring? (engagement, resource access, retention?)

### 3. Safety & Moderation
- How sensitive is anonymity? Should members be able to use aliases?
- Will there be trained moderators or peer supporters, or just admin staff?
- What's the protocol if someone discloses active danger / crisis in a channel?
- Are there legal or ethical guidelines the org already follows (e.g., mandated reporting)?
- Should the server be fully private / invite-only? Verification process?

### 4. Content & Programming
- What wellness topics are priorities? (mindfulness, financial literacy, housing, legal aid, self-care, etc.)
- How often do you want wellness prompts posted? (daily, weekly?)
- Do you have existing content/resources, or are we creating from scratch?
- Are there partner organizations whose resources should be featured?
- What tone should announcements strike? (warm/casual, professional, empowering?)

### 5. Roles & Permissions
- Who are the key roles? (admin, moderator, wellness facilitator, member, new member?)
- Should members progress through trust levels (e.g., new member -> verified -> full access)?
- Will Army Pink staff be identifiable, or blend in?
- Are there external collaborators (therapists, advocates) who need special access?

### 6. Branding & Look
- Do you have specific branding assets (logo, colors, emojis) for the Escape Club?
- Is Escape Club branding separate from Army Pink, or an extension?
- Any specific language preferences or words to avoid? (e.g., "victim" vs "survivor")
- Preferred server name — "Escape Club" or something else?

### 7. Technical & Logistics
- Does anyone on the Army Pink team have Discord experience?
- Who will own/admin the server long-term after volunteer handoff?
- Do you want bot integrations? (welcome bot, moderation bot, anonymous posting?)
- Budget for Discord features? (Nitro boost for custom features, or free tier only?)
- Timeline — when is the target launch date?

---

## Things to Lock In During Meeting 1

These are the decisions that unblock everything else:

- [ ] **Target audience definition** — who exactly is joining
- [ ] **Server access model** — invite-only, application, referral-based?
- [ ] **Safety/crisis protocol direction** — what happens when someone is in danger
- [ ] **Core channel needs** — even a rough list of "we definitely want X, Y, Z"
- [ ] **Role structure** — at minimum: who are the member tiers and staff roles
- [ ] **Branding basics** — server name, tone, any language dos/don'ts
- [ ] **Launch timeline** — hard date or flexible?
- [ ] **Bot preferences** — yes/no on automation
- [ ] **Content they already have** vs what we need to create
- [ ] **Point of contact & check-in cadence** — who do we talk to, how often
